# Module 01: Introduction to AI & Python

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Artificial Intelligence is the broad field of developing computer
systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence,
such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, and making
decisions.

## 2. Machine Learning (ML)
Machine Learning is a subset of AI. It involves training models
on data so they can learn patterns and make predictions on new data.

## 3. Deep Learning (DL)
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses
multilayered neural networks to learn patterns from data.

## 4. Difference Between AI, ML, and DL
- AI is the broad field.
- ML is a subset of AI that learns patterns from data.
- DL is a subset of ML that uses multilayered neural networks.

Relationship: AI > ML > DL.

## 5. Examples of AI Applications
- Understanding and generating language.
- Recognizing patterns in images.
- Making predictions from data.
